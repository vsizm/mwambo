# Mwambo

Know Your Roots. Know Your Zambia.

Mwambo is a Zambian cultural and heritage knowledge platform designed to preserve, organise, and make credible cultural knowledge accessible.

## Project status

Foundation stage. Product architecture and content model are being established before application implementation.

## Principles

- Zambian cultural knowledge first
- Evidence and provenance for important claims
- Human-reviewed content
- Mobile-first, accessible experience
- AI is optional and provider-neutral
- No dependency on AI Studio or Gemini scaffolding

## Planned stack

- Next.js + TypeScript
- PostgreSQL on Neon
- Vercel deployment
- GitHub source control

## Core product areas

1. Heritage & National Identity
2. Marriage, Family & Community

See `docs/` for the product and technical blueprint.
